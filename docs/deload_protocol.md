# Training Intensity Tier Reference

Source: Huberman Lab guest series with Dr. Andy Galpin
("Optimize Your Training Program for Fitness & Longevity")

---

## Core principle

**Volume drives fatigue, not intensity.** When backing off, the first lever to
pull is always volume (sets, duration) — not weight on the bar or pace/zone.
Intensity stays the same across all tiers; only the amount of work changes.

---

## Full tier table

| Tier | Running | Strength |
|------|---------|----------|
| HOLD | Full duration, Zone 2 | Full sets, planned weight |
| REDUCE | Same zone, ~85% duration | Same weight, ~65% of sets |
| DELOAD | Same zone, ~70% duration | Same weight, ~50% of sets |
| SUPPRESS | Zone 1 or skip | Skip or very easy only |

REDUCE and DELOAD differ in magnitude, not type. Both cut volume; neither
cuts intensity.

---

## REDUCE tier

Triggered by a single bad signal (elevated training load, one poor sleep night,
mild HRV dip). Not severe enough for a full deload — just a moderate pullback.

**Running:** Same zone (Zone 2), ~85% of planned duration.
**Strength:** Same weight as planned. Drop to ~65% of normal sets (health-bootcamp protocol: cut 30–40%; was ~80% before 2026-10-16).

## DELOAD tier

Triggered by week 6 of the 6-week cycle, chronic suppression, or well-above
training load. One full week.

**Running:** Same zone (Zone 2), ~70% of planned duration.
**Strength:** Same weight as planned. Drop to ~50% of normal sets (half).

## Timing (DELOAD)

- Every 4–8 weeks (Galpin's most common example: 6-week progressive block → 1 deload week)
- Quarterly structure: ~5 weeks hard → 1 deload week → 5 weeks hard → 1 full rest week

## Run-coach implementation

| Decision | Run | Strength | Jumps |
|----------|-----|----------|-------|
| BUILD | +10% duration, Zone 2 | Full load | As prescribed |
| HOLD | Planned duration, Zone 2 | Full load | As prescribed |
| REDUCE | ~85% duration, Zone 2 | Same weight, ~65% sets | Skipped if HRV/sleep triggered; kept if run load |
| DELOAD | ~70% duration, Zone 2 | Same weight, ~50% sets | Omitted |
| SUPPRESS | Skip or very easy | Skip or very easy | Omitted |
